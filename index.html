Puppy colour predictor

Load the Wisdom Panel genetic report PDF of each parent to see which coat colours their puppies can have, and how likely each one is.

Predictions cover only the variants Wisdom Panel tests. They are not breeding or veterinary advice. Coat colour also changes with age: greying and ticking are common in Spanish Water Dogs and are not predicted here.

Sources

The colour rules follow these studies, databases and laboratory notes. In the health section, condition names and inheritance modes are those Wisdom Panel's reports print.

  • Report layout, allele order and the ayt haplotype: Dreger et al. 2019
  • MC1R: Anderson et al. 2020 (eA), Honkanen et al. 2024 (eH), Dreger and Schmutz 2010 (eG)
  • ASIP: Dreger and Schmutz 2011, Bannasch et al. 2021, Dreger et al. 2020 (ayt phenotype)
  • Saddle tan (RALY): Dreger et al. 2013
  • Red intensity (MFSD12): Hédan et al. 2019
  • Cocoa (HPS3): Kiener et al. 2020
  • Merle (PMEL): Clark et al. 2006, Murphy et al. 2018; harlequin (PSMB7): Clark et al. 2011
  • Roan (USH2A): Brancalion et al. 2021
  • OMIA entries: brown (TYRP1), white spotting, merle, mask, albinism, cocoa (HPS3)
  • Laboratory notes: UC Davis Veterinary Genetics Laboratory (eG), Orivet (eH)
